- 6 feature cards with gradient icons
- Hover animations (scale and lift)

  - Custom gradient icon
  - Clear title
  - Descriptive text

**Features:**
- Multi-Branch Management
- Advanced Analytics
- Inventory Control
- Customer Management
- E-Commerce Ready
- B2B Marketplace

### 7. **Pricing Section**
- 3 pricing tiers
- "Most Popular" badge on middle tier

- Clear pricing in Naira (₦)

**Plans:**

### 9. **Final CTA Section**
- Bold gradient background with grid pattern
- Large, compelling headline

- Company logo and tagline
- Link sections (Product, Company)
